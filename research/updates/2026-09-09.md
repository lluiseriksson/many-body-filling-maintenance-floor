## Source cards

### Restricted typicality in non-equilibrium quantum many-body systems

- Source: [arXiv:2609.07832v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07832v1); Pawlik, Konrad, Sierant, Piotr, Zakrzewski, Jakub.
- Dates: first 2026-09-07; latest 2026-09-07. **new-this-calendar-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Restricted typicality reconstructs nonlinear state diagnostics from slow modes in non-equilibrium many-body systems with separated time scales.
- Limits: Reconstruction of entropy diagnostics is not a uniform quantum recovery channel. Transport and time-scale separation do not prove Davies block invariance or a maintenance exponent.

### Non-Abelian string melting and thermalization in an open lattice gauge theory

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04117v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04117v1); Verde, Erick Parra, Cataldi, Giovanni, Kühn, Stefan, Magnifico, Giuseppe, Halimeh, Jad C..
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: model, thermalization discussion and strong-dephasing reduction.
- Reported result: A tensor-network study couples 1+1D SU(2) gauge theory with matter to a gauge-preserving Lindblad bath. It finds nonmonotonic relaxation and derives an exclusion-process description in a strong-dephasing limit.
- Limits: This is an open-system finite/truncated lattice setting, not pure 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills. A Liouvillian relaxation rate is not automatically a Hamiltonian gap, and the strong-dephasing expansion has its own regime.

### Work Extraction Across a Thermodynamic Hierarchy in Quantum Many-Body Systems

- Source: [arXiv:2608.31001v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31001v1); Hokkyo, Akihiro, Ueda, Masahito.
- Dates: first 2026-08-31; latest 2026-08-31. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Hokkyo and Ueda formulate a hierarchy of allowed operations for work extraction and bound additional work through differences of entropy densities or mutual-information density.
- Limits: An extraction advantage is not an achievable maintenance-power baseline. Specify spatial access, control duration and connectivity. First posted 31 August.
